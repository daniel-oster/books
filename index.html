    We Must Survive Draft

    Analysing, designing, operating and proving that missions survive severe disruption

    In July 2021 a till supplier two tiers away was hit, and 800 Coop stores closed. The regional co-ops that ran their own tills stayed open. Nobody had planned it that way. This book is about planning it that way: deciding what must keep working, finding out what it really depends on, and building the fallbacks before the day you need them. It is written for whoever answers for keeping important services running, with Swedish cases from Tieto 2011 to Tietoevry 2024 and a fictional regional utility as the running example. All 24 chapters, with a tabletop exercise for each, are in a first complete draft.

    Partial Failure

    An introduction to distributed systems, from first principles to production

    You send a request and the timeout fires. Did the write happen? Maybe. This book is about that question and everything that grows out of it. Over seventeen chapters you learn to reason about systems where the network loses messages, clocks drift apart and a server can die halfway through your request, and to build systems that keep working anyway: retries that are safe to repeat, replicas that agree with each other, and services you can change and deploy without waking anyone at three in the morning. Along the way, labs let you break your own code with simulated network partitions and overload, and watch what happens.
